- "کیا یه غذای کیری میخوان" contains a negative quality judgment and a question. It is REWRITE.

Hostile political opinions are not automatically pure abuse. Apply identical criteria to all political sides.

Never invent facts, reverse claims or infer ambiguous identities.
Use REVIEW when classification is genuinely uncertain.
